Small apps I build for problems I have myself, made for anyone who has them too.

  • Start a Company

    For first-time US founders: answer a few questions and get your own checklist for forming a company.

    • Structure, state filings, IRS, bank, licences and yearly upkeep, in order.
    • Fees and deadlines checked against official sources for all 50 states and DC.
    • The official do-it-yourself link always comes first.

  • RamClean

    For Mac users short on memory: spots apps you aren’t using that are eating it, and asks before quitting them.

    • Asks before quitting idle apps, and quits them politely.
    • Learns your answers, so it stops asking about apps you always keep.
    • Warns before your disk fills, and lists rebuildable caches it can move to the Trash.

  • Goal Garden and Dreams

    For anyone with long-term goals: each goal is a plant that grows as you log small steps toward it.

    • Plant a goal as a seed; every step you log waters it.
    • The light follows your clock and the leaves follow the seasons.
    • One garden on your Mac, your iPhone (with a widget) and the web.

    Mac iPhone Web

    Free for 3 goals; Garden Plus $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year planned

  • What To Cook Next

    For beginner cooks: a cooking course that picks one recipe at a time to train your weakest skill.

    • 55 hand-written recipes across 11 skills, leaning toward food you like.
    • Scales each recipe to who you’re cooking for, and sends the list to Instacart or Reminders.
    • Logs every cook, so the next pick is one step harder.

    • Shows who you’ve drifted from, and plans that never got a date.
    • Suggests a message worth sending to each of them.
    • Everything stays on your Mac, in an encrypted vault.

  • Recruiting Platform

    For job seekers in tech and research: matches you to live openings at 136 companies and labs, and explains every ranking.

    • Checks your resume against any posting, and tracks your applications.
    • A browser extension fills in applications, but never submits them for you.
